# Privacy mask

The **privacy mask** hides money amounts on screen so that people near you cannot read them.

## Use it

Tap the **eye** button on **Home**, **Payments**, **Reports** or the staff **history**. Amounts are replaced by hidden symbols. Tap again to show them.

The setting is saved on this device.

## What it does not do

The privacy mask only hides amounts on the screen. It does not lock the app, hide names, or stop anyone who is already signed in. Protect your device with a screen lock and sign out on shared devices.
