# Advertising Policy & Transparency

**Daily Khata Pro — Official Documentation**  
**Effective Date:** October 2026  
**Last Reviewed:** October 2026

This Advertising Policy explains where commercial advertisements may appear in Daily Khata Pro, how third-party advertising providers may operate, what technical information may be processed, and what controls and limitations apply.

Daily Khata Pro is a personal accounting and financial-analysis platform. Advertising is intended to remain separate from core record-keeping workflows as far as the application's implementation allows.

---

## 1.1 Introduction & Scope

This policy defines the principles and technical boundaries governing advertisements displayed within Daily Khata Pro.

Advertising revenue may support hosting, infrastructure, domain maintenance, research, documentation, and continued development.

This document covers advertising placement, third-party providers, delivery, information processed, contextual and personalized advertising, cookies, external links, prohibited content, user controls, children's privacy, technical isolation, and policy revisions.

The exact features, providers, formats, and controls may change between versions. The current application's actual behavior takes precedence over an outdated description.

---

## 1.2 Advertising Placement in the Application

### Primary Dashboard
The primary dashboard, balance cards, and immediate transaction-entry areas are intended to remain focused on bookkeeping.

### Utilities & Content Pages
Depending on the current configuration, advertisements may appear on non-critical utility or content pages such as Financial Calculators, User Manual/Documentation, Settings or informational screens, Attendance-related information, and educational/editorial content.

### Print Statements & PDFs
Advertisements should not be intentionally inserted into exported or printed financial statements, invoices, receipts, or other user financial documents.

---

## 1.3 Third-Party Advertising Providers

Daily Khata Pro may use approved third-party advertising providers. A provider may select and deliver advertisements, measure impressions and interactions, apply frequency controls, and detect invalid or automated traffic.

Third-party providers operate their own infrastructure and privacy policies. The appearance of an advertisement does not mean Daily Khata Pro controls the advertiser's website or data practices.

Advertising providers may change over time, so provider names or technical domains from an older version should not be treated as permanent unless confirmed by the current application.

---

## 1.4 How Advertising Delivery Operates

When a page contains an advertising slot, the browser or application may request advertising content from a third-party server. The provider may select an eligible creative, return it to the designated slot, measure an impression, and process an interaction if the user clicks.

Possible formats include banner, responsive display, native-style recommendation, sponsored-content, or other formats supported by the selected provider.

Daily Khata Pro aims to avoid forced pop-ups, unexpected redirects, deceptive downloads, advertisements imitating application controls, and intrusive overlays. No filtering system can guarantee that every third-party creative will always behave perfectly.

---

## 1.5 Information Processed for Advertising

It is important to distinguish application data from advertising telemetry.

Daily Khata Pro financial records can include income, expenses, balances, notes, categories, goals, attendance/work records, and calculator information. Advertising integration should not be interpreted as permission to send the contents of these records to an advertising provider.

Depending on the advertising technology, third parties may process technical information such as IP address, approximate region, browser/User-Agent, device or platform information, screen or viewport information, referring page, advertisement impressions, clicks, cookies, or similar identifiers.

The exact information depends on the provider, browser, device, consent requirements, and current implementation.

---

## 1.6 Contextual & Personalized Advertising

Contextual advertising may be selected from the general subject of the page being viewed.

Some providers may also use permitted cookies, identifiers, previous interactions, or other signals for more relevant or personalized advertising. Availability depends on the provider, browser and operating-system controls, consent requirements, privacy choices, and current implementation.

Not every advertisement is necessarily personalized, and different users may receive different advertisements.

---

## 1.7 Cookies & Similar Ad Technologies

Third-party advertising partners may use cookies, browser-storage mechanisms, advertising identifiers, pixels, or similar technologies for functions such as frequency control, campaign measurement, fraud prevention, and performance measurement.

The technologies used depend on the advertising provider and current integration. Users should also review the application's Cookies Policy and the provider's privacy documentation.

---

## 1.8 Ad Clicks & External Third-Party Websites

Clicking an advertisement may open an external website, app-store page, landing page, or other third-party destination.

After leaving Daily Khata Pro, the external website controls its own content, privacy practices, cookies, tracking, and security. Users should review the destination's privacy policy, terms, payment conditions, and refund information before submitting personal, financial, or payment information.

---

## 1.9 No Endorsement & Content Representation

An advertisement displayed in Daily Khata Pro does not automatically constitute an endorsement, recommendation, warranty, guarantee, or statement that the advertised product or service is suitable or safe.

Commercial claims belong to the advertiser. Users should independently evaluate products, services, subscriptions, investments, and other commercial offers.

---

## 1.10 Prohibited Content & Quality Filtering

Daily Khata Pro aims to restrict unacceptable advertising through available provider and application controls. This may include malicious software, phishing, deceptive downloads, fraudulent financial schemes, misleading claims, illegal products or services, adult or sexually explicit material, harmful content, deceptive redirects, and advertisements imitating application controls.

If an advertisement appears deceptive, malicious, illegal, or inappropriate, do not interact with it if it appears unsafe. Report it through the available Daily Khata Pro support channel and, where available, the advertising provider's reporting mechanism.

---

## 1.11 User Controls & Privacy Options

Users can control some advertising and tracking behavior through browser and device settings.

**Browser controls:** Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and Edge provide controls for cookies, site permissions, and tracking-related technologies.

**Mobile controls:** Android and iOS provide privacy settings that can affect advertising identifiers and personalized advertising. Menu names may vary by OS version.

**Ad blockers:** Compatible ad-blocking tools may affect advertisement visibility, measurements, page layout, and features that depend on advertising.

**Offline use:** Where offline functionality is available, local record features may continue without an advertising connection; advertising itself may require network access.

---

## 1.12 Children's Privacy & Protection

Daily Khata Pro is intended as a general-purpose financial record and personal finance organization tool. Advertising directed toward minors may be subject to additional legal, consent, platform, and provider requirements.

Daily Khata Pro does not intentionally design advertising features to target minors with inappropriate commercial content. Concerns about inappropriate advertising should be reported through the available support channel.

---

## 1.13 Technical Security & Advertising Isolation

Advertising content is supplied by third-party infrastructure and should be treated as external content. Where technically supported, advertising components should be separated from core financial-record functionality using appropriate browser or application isolation mechanisms.

This separation is intended to reduce unnecessary interaction between advertising content and protected application functionality.

**Important:** Technical isolation should not be described as an absolute guarantee that no third-party script can ever access any browser information. Actual protection depends on the application architecture, browser security model, provider implementation, permissions, and current code. Daily Khata Pro should not intentionally provide advertising providers access to private ledger records merely for advertising purposes.

## Advertising Safety Checklist

Before interacting with an advertisement:

- Check the website address before entering information.
- Be cautious of urgent payment, investment, or prize claims.
- Never share passwords, OTPs, PINs, or recovery codes with an advertiser.
- Do not install unknown software because an advertisement asks you to.
- Review pricing, subscription, cancellation, and refund terms.
- Treat unusually high-return financial claims with caution.
- Verify important commercial offers independently.
- Report advertisements that appear deceptive, malicious, or inappropriate.
