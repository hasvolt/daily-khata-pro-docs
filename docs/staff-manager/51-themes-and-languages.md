# Themes and languages

## Themes

Open **Theme Colors & Mode** from the menu, or **Theme Colors** in the account menu.

| Dark themes | Light themes |
|---|---|
| Emerald Black, Deep Obsidian, Monochrome B&W, Electric Blue, Premium Gold, Sunset Orange, Emerald Green, Royal Violet, Ocean Teal, Crimson Pink | Day White, Daylight Light |

Your choice is remembered on this device. Printouts are always black on a white page.

## Built-in languages

Open **Application Language** in Settings, or **Google Translator & Language** in the menu. Choose from 16 languages that work without internet:

English, Hindi, Urdu, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Odia, Assamese, Nepali, Arabic and Spanish.

- **Urdu** and **Arabic** switch the screen to right-to-left.
- Any text without a translation appears in English.
- Numbers, currency and dates follow your language.

Some text inside the app may still appear in English in other languages. Tell us through Support if you find wrong or missing words.

## Google Translator (online)

**Google Translator (100+)** translates the page into more than 100 languages. It needs internet. The page text is sent to Google Translate. See [Privacy and data security](47-privacy-and-data-security.md).

## Offline

Themes and the 16 built-in languages work without internet.

## Tips

- Use a Light theme in bright light.
- Choose the language of your staff before you print slips.
