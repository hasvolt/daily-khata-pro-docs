# Daily attendance

Open **Attendance** and choose the date (today by default). Only **active** staff are listed.

## Mark attendance

Tap a status for each staff member. Statuses:

| Status | Meaning in calculations |
|---|---|
| **Present** | Full day |
| **Half Day** | Half a day |
| **Absent** | No pay for the day |
| **Paid Leave** | Counted as a payable day |
| **Unpaid Leave** | No pay for the day |
| **Holiday** | Counted as a payable day |

See [Calculations with examples](17-calculations-with-examples.md).

## Mark All Present

**Mark All Present** marks every active staff member who has **no entry yet** as present. Entries you already made are not changed. Then change the exceptions.

## Overtime

Use the overtime buttons on a staff member's row to add or remove overtime hours, one hour at a time.

## Daily summary

The top of the page shows how many staff are Present, Half Day, Absent and on Leave, and the **Total OT Hours** for the day.

## Search

Search by staff name or role to find a person quickly.

## Monthly calendar

Tap the calendar icon on a staff member to open their **monthly calendar**. Days are colour coded for Present, Half Day, Absent, Leave and Holiday. Tap a day to set it to Present, Half, Absent, Paid Leave, Unpaid Leave or Holiday, including for past dates.

## One entry per day

A staff member has one attendance entry per date. Changing the status updates that entry.

## Locked months

If the date is inside a closed month, a banner appears and attendance cannot be changed until the Owner unlocks the month. See [Month settlement and period locking](18-month-settlement-and-period-locking.md).

## Home shortcuts

On **Home** you can also mark staff Present, Half Day or Absent for today.

## Who can mark attendance

Owner, Manager and Supervisor. Accountants and Viewers can only view.

## Offline

Attendance is saved on your device and syncs later.
