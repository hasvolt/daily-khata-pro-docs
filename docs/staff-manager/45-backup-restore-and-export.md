# Backup, restore and export

## Download a backup

Open the menu, choose **Backup & Restore Data**, then **Download Full Backup (.json)**. The file contains your business data: staff profiles, attendance, work entries, payments, sites, work types, custom field definitions, period closings and business settings. Keep it somewhere safe, such as your email or a cloud drive.

You can also export your data from **My Profile & Security**. See [Profile and account settings](53-profile-and-account-settings.md).

## Restore from a backup

1. Open **Backup & Restore Data**.
2. Choose a backup `.json` file.
3. The app shows a **Backup File Preview** of what is inside.
4. Choose how to restore:
   - **Merge:** adds the backup records to the current business.
   - **New Business:** creates a new business from the backup. Give it a **New Business Name**.
5. Confirm.

If the file is not a Staff Manager backup, the app shows **Not a valid Staff Manager backup JSON file**.

## Good habits

- Download a backup before importing, upgrading to cloud, deleting staff or deleting your account.
- Download a backup at least once a month.
- Try a restore into a **New Business** once so you know it works.

## Cloud data

If you use a cloud account, your administrator is responsible for database backups. See [Deployment guide](80-deployment-guide.md).
