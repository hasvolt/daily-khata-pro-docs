# Profile and account settings

Open **My Profile & Security** from the account menu.

## Your details

- **Email** (shown, cannot be changed here)
- **Full Name**
- **Phone Number (Optional)**

Choose **Save Profile**. Your saved name and phone appear again when you reopen the profile.

## Change password

Enter a **new password** (at least 8 characters) and choose **Update**.

## Sign out

**Sign Out** ends this device's session. **Sign Out All Devices** ends all your sessions.

## Backup / Export All Data

**Export** downloads a JSON copy of your business records. See [Backup, restore and export](45-backup-restore-and-export.md).

## Delete account and cloud data

**Delete Account & Associated Cloud Data** permanently deletes your account and the businesses you own that have no other members.

1. Export your data first.
2. Choose the delete option.
3. Confirm the second warning.

If you own a business that has other members, transfer ownership or remove the members first. Deleted cloud data cannot be recovered.

## Local mode

Without an account the profile screen shows **Running in Local Device Mode**. See [Local mode and upgrade to cloud](04-local-mode-and-upgrade-to-cloud.md).
