# Salary slips and statements

## Salary slip

Open a staff member and choose **Salary Slip**, or open it from the Payments page or the history.

1. Choose the **month** or the dates.
2. Optionally edit the **Custom Header Note** (for example a registration number) and the **Custom Footer Note**. They start with the print header and footer saved in [Business settings](50-business-settings.md).
3. Choose whether to **include signatures** and whether to **include the staff photo**.
4. Print, download or share.

The slip shows the business name, the staff details, the period, the earnings breakdown (including overtime), bonuses, reimbursements, deductions, advances, salary paid and the **Net Payable Balance**. If signatures are included, it has lines for **Authorized Signatory** and **Employee / Staff Signature**.

## Output options

| Option | Result |
|---|---|
| **Print** | Opens the print dialog |
| **PDF** | Downloads a PDF salary slip |
| **CSV** | Downloads the slip figures as a CSV file |
| **Share** | Shares a text summary |

See [Print, PDF, Excel and share](33-print-pdf-excel-and-share.md) for details and for the PDF language limit.

## Payment receipt

From the Payments list, open the receipt for a payment. It shows a receipt number, the staff member, date, payment mode and amount paid, with a **Received By** signature line. You can print, download a PDF or share it.

## Amounts depend on records

A slip is calculated from the attendance, work and payment records at the time you create it. If you change records or the rate later, a new slip can show different figures. Save a PDF of each month's slip after you settle the month.

## Who can create them

Roles that can view payments: Owner, Manager and Accountant. See [Roles and permissions](41-roles-and-permissions.md).
