# Quick start

## 1. Open the app

Go to `https://staff.rozfiber.com`. You can [install it](05-install-and-offline-use.md) like an app.

## 2. Sign in, or start in Local mode

- Tap **Sign In**, then **Sign up now**. Enter your full name, email and a password (at least 8 characters), then confirm your email using the message we send you.
- Or use the app without an account in [Local mode](04-local-mode-and-upgrade-to-cloud.md) and upgrade later. Local mode must be enabled by the administrator.

## 3. Create your business

Enter a business name, choose a business type, a default currency and the day your week starts, then create it. You become the **Owner**. See [Businesses](10-businesses.md).

## 4. Add your staff

Open **Staff** and add each person: name, role, payment type (Daily Wage, Monthly Fixed Salary, Hourly Rate or Per Piece / Per Unit) and rate. See [Staff profiles](11-staff-profiles.md).

## 5. Mark today's attendance

Open **Attendance**. Tap **Mark All Present** to mark everyone who has no entry yet, then change the exceptions. See [Daily attendance](14-daily-attendance.md).

## 6. Record work (optional)

From **Home**, use **Log Daily Work** to note what was done and where. See [Work entries, sites and work types](15-work-entries-sites-work-types.md).

## 7. Record a payment

Open **Payments** and choose **Record Payment**. Pick the staff member, type, mode, amount and date. See [Payments, advances and ledger](16-payments-advances-and-ledger.md).

## 8. Print a statement

Open **Reports**, choose a report and dates, then print, download or share. See [Reports and filters](32-reports-and-filters.md).

## Good habits

- Mark attendance every day.
- Record advances on the day you give them.
- Settle and lock each month. See [Month settlement and period locking](18-month-settlement-and-period-locking.md).
- Download a backup regularly. See [Backup, restore and export](45-backup-restore-and-export.md).
