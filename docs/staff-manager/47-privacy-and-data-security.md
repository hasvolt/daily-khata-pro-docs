# Privacy and data security

## What is stored

Staff names and contact details, attendance, work and payment records, optional photos and notes, and your business information.

Do not enter government ID numbers, full bank account numbers or card numbers. The **ID proof note** and **Payment Note / UPI ID** fields are free text and are not a safe place for such details.

## Where it is stored

- On your device, in your browser's storage.
- If you sign in, in your cloud account.

## Who can see it

Only the members of your business, and only what their role allows. See [Roles and permissions](41-roles-and-permissions.md). Pay rates and payment records are not sent to Supervisors.

## Protections

- The database checks access for every request, so one business cannot read another's data.
- Connections use HTTPS.
- Passwords are handled by the sign-in service and are not stored in the app.
- Changes to important records are written to an activity log.
- The site sends security headers.

No system can promise perfect security. Use a strong password, keep your devices locked and sign out on shared devices.

## Your controls

- Hide amounts with the [privacy mask](52-app-lock-and-privacy-mask.md).
- Choose what appears in reports. For example, phone numbers appear only if you include them.
- Download your data at any time. See [Backup, restore and export](45-backup-restore-and-export.md).
- Delete your account and cloud data. See [Profile and account settings](53-profile-and-account-settings.md).

## Online translation

If you use **Google Translator**, the page text is sent to Google Translate to be translated. Turn it off if this is not acceptable for your business. The 16 built-in languages work without it.

## Your responsibility

You are responsible for telling your staff what records you keep and for following the laws that apply to you. See the app's **Privacy Policy** and **Terms of Service**.
