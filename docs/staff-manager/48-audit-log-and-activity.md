# Activity and audit log

Open the menu and choose **Activity & Audit Log**.

## What it shows

A list of changes to your business records, with the type of change, who made it and when.

## Filter

Filter by:

- All Activities
- Staff Changes
- Attendance
- Payments
- Period Settlements
- Business Settings

## Why it matters

- Find out who changed an amount or an attendance entry.
- Review month locks and unlocks.
- See how conflicts were resolved.

## Who can see it

The audit log is available to the Owner, Manager and Accountant. It needs a cloud account.
