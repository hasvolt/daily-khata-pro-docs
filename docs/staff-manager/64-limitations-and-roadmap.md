# Limitations and roadmap

## Not included

- Tax, GST, PF, ESI and statutory payroll calculations
- Biometric or location based attendance
- Bank transfers or salary payment through the app
- Advanced calculators (use Daily Khata Pro)
- Push notifications
- Importing attendance or payments (only staff CSV import)
- Paper size and column choices when printing

## Known limits in version 1.0.0

| Area | Limit |
|---|---|
| **Rate changes** | The Rate Revision History is recorded, but calculations use the current rate for all periods. Settle and save slips before changing a rate |
| **Month mode** | The Month Settlement screen has its own selector that starts at Fixed 30 days. Reports and slips use the Settings value (default Actual Calendar Days) |
| **PDF languages** | PDF downloads use a Latin font. Hindi, Urdu, Tamil and other scripts may not display correctly. Use Print or CSV |
| **Terminology and custom labels** | Terminology Preset, Custom Site / Project Label and Custom Work / Task Label are saved but do not change screen labels yet |
| **Owner approval for deleting staff** | The setting is saved but is not enforced yet |
| **App lock (PIN)** | Not available. Use your device screen lock |
| **Custom fields** | Available for staff profiles only |
| **Local mode** | Data stays on one device. No team features |
| **Cloud upgrade** | Copies staff, attendance and payments |
| **Accounts** | Staff Manager and Daily Khata Pro have separate accounts |
| **Online translation** | Needs internet and sends page text to Google Translate |

## Ideas for the future

- Past rates applied by effective date
- A shared sign-in across Rozfiber apps
- Automatic transfer of staff payments to Daily Khata Pro
- Unicode fonts in PDF downloads
- More reports and charts
- Notifications and reminders

Roadmap items may change.
