# Accounts and sign in

An account lets you keep your data in the cloud, use more than one device and invite team members.

## Create an account

1. Tap **Sign In**, then **Sign up now**.
2. Enter your full name, your email and a password of at least 8 characters.
3. The app tells you that the account was created and asks you to check your email.
4. Open the email and tap the confirmation link.
5. Return to the app and sign in.

Until the email is confirmed, signing in shows **Email not confirmed**. Check your Spam folder and wait a few minutes. The email service limits how many messages can be sent, so avoid creating several accounts in a row.

## Ways to sign in

| Method | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Email and password** | The normal way |
| **Magic link** | Choose *Sign in with Magic Link*. A sign-in link is sent to your email |
| **Google** | Shown only if the administrator enabled it |

## Forgot password

Tap **Forgot password?**, enter your email and follow the link in the message.

## Change password

Open **My Profile & Security**, enter a new password (minimum 8 characters) and tap **Update**.

## Sign out

- **Sign Out** ends the session on this device.
- **Sign Out All Devices** ends the sessions on every device.

## Delete your account

In **My Profile & Security**, choose **Delete Account & Associated Cloud Data**. The app first recommends exporting your data, then asks for a second confirmation. If you own a business that still has other members, transfer ownership first. Deleted cloud data cannot be recovered. See [Profile and account settings](53-profile-and-account-settings.md).

## Local mode

You can also use the app without an account. See [Local mode and upgrade to cloud](04-local-mode-and-upgrade-to-cloud.md).
