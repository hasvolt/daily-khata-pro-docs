# Staff Manager Documentation

**Staff Manager** is an offline-first staff, attendance and payment record-keeping app by Rozfiber. It is available at [staff.rozfiber.com](https://staff.rozfiber.com) and works alongside **Daily Khata Pro**.

> This documentation applies to Staff Manager **version 1.0.0** and was last reviewed against the app on 10 October 2026.

## What is Staff Manager?

Staff Manager lets you add staff once, mark daily attendance, record work, payments and advances, see each person's balance, close and lock a month, and print or download salary slips and reports. Your data is saved on your device first, so you can keep working without internet. If you sign in, it also syncs to your cloud account and can be shared with your team.

## Who is it for?

Contractors, shop owners, workshops and small factories, farms and households that pay staff daily, monthly, hourly or per piece.

## Important product information

- **Offline use:** after the first visit the app opens without internet. Changes sync when you are online. See [Multi-device, sync and offline](42-multi-device-sync-and-offline.md).
- **Accounts and cloud:** an account is needed for several devices and team members. Local mode needs no account but keeps data on one device. See [Accounts and sign in](03-accounts-and-sign-in.md).
- **Reports and exports:** print, PDF, CSV and share. PDF downloads use a Latin font. See [Print, PDF, Excel and share](33-print-pdf-excel-and-share.md).
- **Privacy and security:** access depends on each member's role. See [Privacy and data security](47-privacy-and-data-security.md) and [Roles and permissions](41-roles-and-permissions.md).
- **Limitations:** see [Limitations and roadmap](64-limitations-and-roadmap.md).

## Integrations

Staff Manager and Daily Khata Pro are separate apps with separate accounts. Staff Manager links to Daily Khata Pro, passes amounts to its advanced calculators and can export payments as CSV. See [Daily Khata Pro integration](70-daily-khata-pro-integration.md).

## Important disclaimer

Staff Manager is a record-keeping tool. It does not replace payroll, tax or legal compliance advice. You are responsible for the accuracy of your entries and for following the labour and tax rules that apply to you.
