# Reconcile a staff balance

Use these steps if a balance or total looks wrong.

1. **Check the business.** Make sure the right business is selected in the header.
2. **Check the staff member and dates.** Open the **Staff Ledger** and choose the period. Use **This Month**, **Last Month**, **Custom** or **All**.
3. **Check the pay type and rate.** Open the staff details. Remember that past periods use the current rate. See [Payment types and rates](12-payment-types-and-rates.md).
4. **Check attendance.** Open the monthly calendar. Look for wrong statuses and missing days.
5. **Check work entries.** For hourly and per piece staff, hours and quantities drive the earnings.
6. **Check payments.** In the ledger, review each wage, advance, bonus, deduction, penalty, reimbursement, loan and repayment. Check dates and amounts.
7. **Check the month mode.** Settlement, reports and slips can use different month modes. See [Calculations with examples](17-calculations-with-examples.md).
8. **Check deleted items.** Open **Trash**. A record deleted by mistake can be restored.
9. **Compare the formula breakdown** in the ledger with your own arithmetic. You can use the [basic calculator](20-basic-calculator.md).

## Avoid counting twice

An advance is a separate payment from salary. Advances are recovered in the net payable formula. Do not also enter the recovery as a deduction.

## Correct at the source

Correct the original attendance, work or payment record rather than adding an unrelated payment to compensate. Check the ledger and slip again after any correction.

## Locked months

If the month is locked, ask the Owner to unlock it before correcting records. Lock it again after the correction.

## Still not matching?

Download the [salary slip](31-salary-slips-and-statements.md) and the [Full Staff History report](32-reports-and-filters.md), and compare them line by line.
