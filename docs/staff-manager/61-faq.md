# FAQ

**Do I need an account?**
Not necessarily. If the administrator has enabled Local mode you can start without one. A cloud account is needed for several devices and team members.

**Does it work without internet?**
Yes, after the first visit. Changes sync when you are online again. Signing in, joining a business and online translation need internet.

**Is there a limit on staff?**
The app does not set a fixed limit.

**Can my manager or supervisor use it on their phone?**
Yes. Invite them from **Team Members & Roles**.

**Can a supervisor see salaries?**
No. Supervisors do not receive pay rates or payments.

**I deleted something by mistake.**
Open **Trash** and restore it.

**Can I change a rate without changing past months?**
Not in version 1.0.0. Past periods use the current rate. Settle the month and save the salary slips first. See [Payment types and rates](12-payment-types-and-rates.md).

**Why does my PDF not show Hindi correctly?**
The PDF download uses a standard Latin font. Use **Print** and save as PDF from your browser, or use CSV. See [Print, PDF, Excel and share](33-print-pdf-excel-and-share.md).

**Does it calculate tax, PF or ESI?**
No. It is a record-keeping tool. Follow the rules that apply to you.

**Can I use it in my language?**
There are 16 built-in languages, and Google Translator for more online.

**Can I import attendance or payments?**
No. You can import a staff list from CSV.

**How do I get my data out?**
Download a backup JSON, or use CSV exports from reports.

**Who can see my data?**
Only members of your business, based on their role.

**Does it connect to Daily Khata Pro?**
It links to Daily Khata Pro, passes amounts to its calculators and can export payments as CSV. They are separate apps with separate accounts. See [Daily Khata Pro integration](70-daily-khata-pro-integration.md).
