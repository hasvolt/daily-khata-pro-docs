# Work history

Open a staff member and choose **Work & Payment History**.

## What you see

A timeline that combines:

- Attendance
- Work entries, with sites and work types
- Payments, advances and adjustments

A summary shows the totals for the chosen period, such as days present, half days, overtime hours and payments.

## Filters

- **Date range:** quick ranges or custom start and end dates. There is no one-month limit.
- **Type:** All, Attendance, Work or Payment.
- **Site:** all sites or one site.

## Actions

From the history you can:

- **Print** the history
- Download a **PDF**
- Download a **CSV**
- **Share** a summary
- Open the staff member's **Salary Slip**

Amounts follow the [privacy mask](52-privacy-mask.md).
