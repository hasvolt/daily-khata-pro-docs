# Business settings

Open **Settings**. Only the **Owner** can change business settings. Choose **Save Business Settings** after your changes.

## Business details

| Setting | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Business Name** | Shown in the header and on printouts |
| **Phone** | Business phone |
| **Default Currency** | INR, USD, AED, SAR, EUR, GBP, PKR, BDT or NPR |
| **Address** | Business address |

## Monthly salary calculation mode

Choose how monthly salaries are prorated:

- **Actual Calendar Days in Month** (default)
- **Fixed 30 Days Month Standard**

Reports, salary slips and history use this setting. The Month Settlement screen has its own selector, so keep it consistent. See [Calculations with examples](17-calculations-with-examples.md).

## Print text

- **Default Print Header**
- **Default Print Footer**

These start in the salary slip's header and footer notes, which you can edit for each slip. See [Salary slips and statements](31-salary-slips-and-statements.md).

## Team options

- **Hide payment amounts from Viewers**

## Saved but not yet applied

The settings screen also contains **Terminology Preset**, **Custom Site / Project Label**, **Custom Work / Task Label** and **Require Owner Approval to Delete Staff with History**. They are saved with the business, but in version 1.0.0 they do not yet change screen labels or deletion rules. See [Limitations and roadmap](64-limitations-and-roadmap.md).

## Theme and language

Theme and language are personal settings on your device. See [Themes and languages](51-themes-and-languages.md).

## Trash

The Owner chooses auto-purge for Trash on the Trash page. See [Trash and recovery](44-trash-and-recovery.md).
