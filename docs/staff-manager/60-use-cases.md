# Use cases

## Contractor or construction

- Add a **site** for each building or customer.
- Add staff such as mistri, helper and electrician. Most are on **Daily Wage**.
- Use **Per Piece / Per Unit** for work paid by quantity (for example per sq ft) and record the quantity in work entries.
- Let a **Supervisor** mark attendance on site. They do not see wages.
- Settle and lock each month.

## Shop or showroom

- Use **Monthly Fixed Salary** for regular staff and **Daily Wage** for helpers.
- Use **Paid Leave** and **Unpaid Leave**.
- Record **advances** and see them recovered in the net balance.

## Workshop or small factory

- Mix **Daily Wage**, **Hourly Rate** and **Per Piece** staff.
- Record overtime hours.
- Use work types and the Work Summary report.

## Farm

- Use sites as fields and work types such as harvesting.
- Use per piece pay with a unit such as bag or kg.

## Household

- Record the monthly salary of a cook, driver or guard.
- Record advances and bonuses.
- Print a salary slip each month.

## Several businesses

One account can hold many businesses. Switch from the header.

## Every business

1. Add staff and rates first.
2. Mark attendance daily.
3. Record advances when you give them.
4. Settle and lock at month end.
5. Download a backup every month.
