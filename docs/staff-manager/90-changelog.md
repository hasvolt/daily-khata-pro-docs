# Changelog

## Version 1.0.0

Features available in this version:

- Accounts with email and password, email confirmation, password reset, magic link and optional Google sign-in
- Local mode and upgrade to cloud
- Several businesses per account, with a business switcher
- Staff profiles with Daily Wage, Monthly Fixed Salary, Hourly Rate and Per Piece / Per Unit pay
- Custom fields for staff
- Staff import from CSV with preview and rollback
- Daily attendance, Mark All Present, overtime and monthly calendar
- Work entries, sites and work types
- Payments, advances, bonuses, deductions, penalties, reimbursements, loans and repayments
- Staff ledger, salary slips and payment receipts
- Month settlement and period locking
- Reports with filters and saved presets
- Print, PDF, CSV and share
- Team members, invites and five roles
- Trash with restore, auto-purge and permanent delete
- Activity and audit log, sync conflicts and backup and restore
- 12 themes, 16 built-in languages and online Google Translator
- Offline PWA with install support
- Basic calculator with a link to the Daily Khata Pro calculators
- Daily Khata Pro link and payment export (CSV)

See [Limitations and roadmap](64-limitations-and-roadmap.md) for known limits in this version.
