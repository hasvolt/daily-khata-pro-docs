# Dashboard

**Home** gives a quick view of your business.

## Today at a Glance

- **Total Staff**
- **Present Today**
- **Absent Today**

## Quick actions

**Today's Attendance**, **Make Payment**, **Add Staff** and **View Reports**.

On a new business the app also shows a **3-Step Quick Start Guide**.

## Mark attendance from Home

Mark staff **Present**, **Half Day** or **Absent** for today directly from Home.

## Manage and log

**Manage Sites / Shops**, **Manage Work Types** and **Log Daily Work** open from Home.

## Cost summaries

- **Cost by Site / Shop:** payments grouped by the staff member's default site, with **Unassigned** for staff without one.
- **Cost by Role:** payments grouped by role.
- **Monthly Payout Trend:** payments over the last four months.

These are summaries of payment records. Open the Payments list or Reports to see the records behind a number.

## Privacy mask

Use the eye button to hide amounts on screen. See [Privacy mask](52-privacy-mask.md).

## Daily Khata Pro

The **Daily Khata Pro Integration** card links to Daily Khata Pro and can export your payouts as a CSV. See [Daily Khata Pro integration](70-daily-khata-pro-integration.md).

## Sync status

The header badge shows **Synced**, **Syncing**, **Offline** or **Sync Issue**. See [Multi-device, sync and offline](42-multi-device-sync-and-offline.md).
