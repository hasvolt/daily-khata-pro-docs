# Navigation and screen layout

## Bottom navigation

The bottom bar has five sections: **Home**, **Staff**, **Attendance**, **Payments** and **Reports**.

## Header

The header contains the menu button, the business switcher, the sync status badge, a calculator shortcut and your account menu (or **Sign In** if you are not signed in).

The account menu includes **My Profile & Security**, **Theme Colors**, **Google Translator**, **Settings** and **Sign Out**.

## Side menu

The menu opens from the header and includes:

- Create Another Business
- Team Members & Roles
- Sync Conflicts
- Activity & Audit Log
- Backup & Restore Data
- Settings
- Trash (Recycle Bin)
- Help Center
- Theme Colors & Mode
- Google Translator & Language
- Inbuilt Quick Calculator
- Open Daily Khata Pro and Advanced Calculators
- About Staff Manager, Privacy Policy, Terms of Service and Contact Support

Some items depend on whether you are signed in and on your role.

## Home

Home shows your business name and:

- **Today at a Glance**: Total Staff, Present Today, Absent Today
- **Primary Quick Actions**: Today's Attendance, Make Payment, Add Staff and View Reports
- A **3-Step Quick Start Guide**
- Quick buttons to mark staff present, half day or absent
- **Manage Sites / Shops**, **Manage Work Types** and **Log Daily Work**
- **Cost by Site / Shop**, **Cost by Role** and **Monthly Payout Trend**
- The **Daily Khata Pro Integration** card
- A privacy mask button to hide amounts

See [Dashboard](19-dashboard.md).

## Other pages

`Staff`, `Attendance`, `Payments`, `Reports`, `Settings`, `Trash`, `Help`, `About`, `Privacy` and `Terms` are separate pages. Unknown addresses show a **Page Not Found** screen with a **Back to Home** button.

## Tips

- Check the business name in the header before editing, paying or deleting.
- If a save is pending, wait for the sync badge before repeating the action.
