# Businesses

A **business** is one workplace with its own staff, records, settings and team. One account can belong to several businesses, for example "Shop A" and "Construction Site".

## Create a business

Choose **Create business** (or **Create Another Business** in the menu) and fill in:

| Field | Options |
|---|---|
| **Business / Shop Name** | Required |
| **Business Type Preset** | Construction / Contractor, Retail Shop / Store, Factory / Workshop, Farm / Agriculture, Household / Domestic Staff, General / Other Business. It is saved with the business |
| **Default Currency** | INR, USD, AED, SAR, EUR, GBP, PKR, BDT, NPR |
| **Week Starts On** | Monday, Sunday or Saturday |

You become the **Owner** of the business.

## Switch business

Use the business switcher in the header. The business you select is the one you see and edit, so check it before editing, paying or deleting.

## Join a business

1. Ask the Owner or a Manager for a **12-character invite code**.
2. Choose **Join business** and enter the code.
3. You join with the role chosen in the invite. See [Roles and permissions](41-roles-and-permissions.md).

Invite codes expire and have a limited number of uses. See [Team members and invites](40-team-members-and-invites.md).

## Business settings

The Owner can change the business name, phone, address, month calculation mode, print text and team options. See [Business settings](50-business-settings.md).

## Leave or delete a business

Owners cannot leave a business that has other members. Transfer ownership first. Deleting your account deletes businesses you own that have no other members. See [Profile and account settings](53-profile-and-account-settings.md).
