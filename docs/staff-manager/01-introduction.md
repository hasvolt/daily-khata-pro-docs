# Introduction

**Staff Manager** is an offline-first staff, attendance and payment record-keeping app for local business owners, contractors, shops, workshops, farms and households. It is available at `https://staff.rozfiber.com` and is a sister product of **Daily Khata Pro**.

## What you can do

- Add each staff member once, with a pay type and rate.
- Mark daily attendance in a few taps, including half days, leave, holidays and overtime.
- Record what work was done and where (site, shop or project).
- Record payments, advances, bonuses, deductions, penalties, reimbursements and loans.
- See each staff member's ledger and net balance.
- Close and lock a month after settlement.
- Print, download or share salary slips, payment receipts and reports.
- Invite managers, supervisors, accountants and viewers to work on the same business.
- Keep working without internet. Changes sync when you are back online.

## Who it is for

Contractors, shop owners, workshops and small factories, farms, and households that pay staff daily, monthly, hourly or per piece. Currency, language and week start are configurable.

## Main ideas

| Idea | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Business** | One workplace with its own staff, records and team. One account can have several businesses. |
| **Staff** | A person you pay. You add them once. |
| **Attendance** | Present, absent, half day, paid leave, unpaid leave or holiday, plus overtime hours, for a date. |
| **Work entry** | A note of what a staff member did on a date, optionally with site, work type, quantity and hours. |
| **Payment** | Money paid or an adjustment: salary, advance, bonus, deduction, penalty, reimbursement, loan or loan repayment. |
| **Ledger** | Earned, paid, advances and balance for one staff member. |
| **Period lock** | Closes a month so attendance and payments in it cannot be changed. |
| **Trash** | Where deleted items wait so they can be restored. |

## Where your data lives

Everything you enter is saved on your device first. If you sign in, changes are also sent to your cloud account in the background. See [Multi-device, sync and offline](42-multi-device-sync-and-offline.md) and [Privacy and data security](47-privacy-and-data-security.md).

## Important

Staff Manager is a record-keeping tool. It does not replace payroll, tax or legal compliance advice. You are responsible for the accuracy of your entries and for following the labour and tax rules that apply to you.
