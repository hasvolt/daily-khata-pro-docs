# Trash and recovery

Deleting does not erase a record immediately.

## What goes to Trash

Staff, attendance entries, work entries, payments, sites, work types and custom fields.

## Open Trash

Open the menu and choose **Trash (Recycle Bin)**.

## Restore

Choose **Restore** on an item. The item returns as it was. Restoring a staff member also brings back the records that were moved with them.

## Delete forever

Only the **Owner** can delete permanently. The app asks you to type `DELETE` to confirm. This cannot be undone.

## Empty Trash

The Owner can empty the whole Trash with a confirmation.

## Auto-purge

The Owner can choose when old items are removed from Trash: **Never (Keep Forever)**, **After 30 Days**, **After 60 Days** or **After 90 Days**. The default is to keep items forever.

## Archive instead of delete

For staff who left, use **Archive**. See [Staff profiles](11-staff-profiles.md).

## Other safety nets

- Locked months protect attendance and payments. See [Month settlement and period locking](18-month-settlement-and-period-locking.md).
- The [activity log](48-audit-log-and-activity.md) records changes.
- Download [backups](45-backup-restore-and-export.md) regularly.
