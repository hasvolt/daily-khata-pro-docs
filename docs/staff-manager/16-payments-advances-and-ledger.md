# Payments, advances and ledger

Open **Payments**.

## Payment types

| Type | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Wage / Salary** | Salary or wages paid to the staff member |
| **Advance (Peshgi / Udhar)** | Money given before it is earned |
| **Bonus / Incentive** | Extra amount owed to the staff member |
| **Deduction** | An amount subtracted |
| **Penalty / Fine** | A fine subtracted |
| **Reimbursement** | Money the staff member spent for the business and you repay |
| **Loan to Staff** | A loan given to the staff member |
| **Loan Repayment** | A repayment of an advance or loan |

## Record a payment

Choose **Record Payment** and fill in:

- **Staff Member**, **Payment Type**, **Payment Mode** (Cash, UPI, Bank Transfer, Cheque, Other), **Amount** and **Payment Date**
- **Period Covered (Optional)**: from and to dates
- **Reference / Transaction ID / Bank Note**
- **Notes & Remarks**
- **Payment Receipt / Voucher Photo (Optional)**

You can pay in parts and make several payments for the same period. The amount box has a calculator icon.

## Duplicate warning

If a payment with the same amount and date already exists for that staff member, the app shows a **Duplicate Payment Warning**. Save only after you confirm that the new payment is real.

## Locked months

If the payment date is inside a closed month, the payment cannot be recorded or edited until the Owner unlocks the month.

## Payments list

**All Transactions** lists payments. Search by staff, note or reference, and filter by type, staff and mode. For each payment you can print or share a **receipt**, edit it, or delete it to Trash. See [Salary slips and statements](31-salary-slips-and-statements.md).

## Staff Ledger

Open a staff member's **Staff Ledger** to see:

- **Total Earned** for the chosen dates
- **Salary Paid**
- **Advances O/S** (outstanding advances and loans)
- The **formula breakdown** for the earnings
- All payment and advance transactions

Filter the ledger by **This Month**, **Last Month**, **Custom** dates or **All**, and by transaction type.

## Balance wording

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Owed to Staff** | You still owe the staff member |
| **Owed by Staff (Advance)** | The staff member has taken more than earned |
| **Settled** | Nothing is due either way |

Read the label and the amount, not the colour alone. How the balance is calculated is explained in [Calculations with examples](17-calculations-with-examples.md).

## Who can use it

| Role | Payments |
|---|---|
| Owner, Manager, Accountant | Record, edit, delete, view |
| Viewer | View only, unless the Owner hides payment amounts from Viewers |
| Supervisor | No access |
