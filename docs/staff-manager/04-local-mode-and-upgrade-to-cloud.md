# Local mode and upgrade to cloud

## Local mode

If the administrator has enabled it, you can use Staff Manager without an account. Your business is created on this device and stays there. The profile screen shows **Running in Local Device Mode**.

Local mode is good for trying the app or for very small, single-device use.

Limits of Local mode:

- Your data exists only in this browser. Clearing browser data or losing the device can remove it.
- There is no cloud copy, no team members and no second device.

Download a backup regularly. See [Backup, restore and export](45-backup-restore-and-export.md).

## Upgrade to cloud

1. Create an account and sign in. See [Accounts and sign in](03-accounts-and-sign-in.md).
2. Choose the **upgrade to cloud** option offered by the app.
3. The app creates a cloud business and moves your local staff, attendance and payments to it. They are queued for sync.
4. Stay online until the sync badge shows **Synced**.

The upgrade copies staff, attendance entries and payments. After upgrading, check that sites, work types and settings look correct, and download a fresh backup.

## Tips

- Download a backup before upgrading.
- Do not clear browser data until sync is complete.
