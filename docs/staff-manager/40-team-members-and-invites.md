# Team members and invites

Several people can work on the same business, each on their own device. Team features need a cloud account and internet.

## Open the team screen

Open the menu and choose **Team Members & Roles**. It has three tabs: **Members**, **Invites** and **Devices**.

## Invite someone

1. Open the **Invites** tab.
2. Choose a **role**: Manager, Supervisor, Accountant or Viewer.
3. For a Supervisor you can limit access to chosen **sites**.
4. Choose how many people can use the invite: 1, 5 or 20.
5. Create the invite, then **Copy Link** or share the **12-character code**.

Invites expire after 7 days by default and can be revoked before they are used.

The person signs in (or creates an account), chooses **Join business** and enters the code.

## Who can invite whom

| You are | You can invite |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Manager, Supervisor, Accountant, Viewer |
| **Manager** | Supervisor, Accountant, Viewer (not Managers) |

## Members tab

The list shows each member, their role and their status.

- **Change role:** Owner only.
- **Remove:** the Owner can remove members. A Manager can remove Supervisors, Accountants and Viewers. The Owner cannot be removed.
- **Transfer ownership:** the Owner can make another member the Owner. The previous Owner becomes a Manager. A business always has an Owner.

A removed member loses access to the business data.

## Devices tab

Shows the devices that have used the business and when they were last seen.
