# Getting Started with Daily Khata Pro

Welcome to Daily Khata Pro. This guide introduces the documentation and gives you a practical starting point for learning about the app.

## 1. What Is Daily Khata Pro?

Daily Khata Pro is designed to help users organize financial records and related information. Depending on the installed version, available tools may include income and expense records, notes, budgets, goals, calculators, reports, work-related tracking, and backup options.

The features and labels you see can vary by version. Use the controls available in your installed app, and consult the relevant chapter for detailed instructions.

## 2. Start with the Basics

If you are new to the app, follow this general sequence:

1. Open the app and become familiar with its main navigation.
2. Review the available sections before entering information.
3. Decide which records you want to maintain, such as personal spending, work expenses, or other financial activity.
4. When adding a record, carefully check its date, amount, category, and description.
5. Review saved entries and any available summaries to make sure the information is correct.
6. Learn how the app stores data and what backup options are available in your version.

This is a general orientation guide. The exact steps for a specific screen are covered in the relevant feature chapter.

## 3. Understand Records and Calculators

A saved financial record and a calculator result serve different purposes.

- **Records** help you keep track of information you enter, such as income or expenses.
- **Calculators** help you calculate or estimate a value based on the inputs and assumptions you provide.

A calculation does not automatically create a financial record unless the app provides a specific action to save or transfer the result. After using a calculator, check whether you need to record the real transaction separately.

## 4. Enter Information Carefully

For useful records:

- Use the correct date.
- Check the amount before saving.
- Choose a category that accurately describes the activity, where categories are available.
- Write a description that will still make sense when you review it later.
- Avoid creating duplicate entries.
- Correct mistakes using the editing or correction options available in your version.

For important amounts, compare app entries with receipts, statements, invoices, or other original documents.

## 5. Review Your Records

Review your entries regularly. Depending on the features available, you may be able to examine transaction history, summaries, charts, budgets, goals, or reports.

When checking a summary, confirm the date range and included records. A summary can only reflect the information entered into the app; it does not independently verify that a payment or other real-world event occurred.

## 6. Protect Your Information

Financial records and personal notes may contain sensitive information.

- Use appropriate device security and any app-lock feature available in your version.
- Do not share your passcode or recovery information.
- Avoid storing passwords or highly sensitive credentials in general notes.
- Be careful when exporting or sharing files.
- Understand where your records are stored before clearing app data, reinstalling the app, or changing devices.

A lock screen alone does not prove that all stored information is encrypted. Protection depends on the app's actual implementation and the device or storage environment.

## 7. Learn About Backup and Restore

Before relying on the app for important records, read the backup and restore guidance for your version.

If a backup or export option is available:

1. Create a backup using the supported process.
2. Store the resulting file somewhere safe.
3. Keep an untouched copy of important backups.
4. Before restoring, check whether the operation could replace existing records.
5. Verify the restored information before deleting any other copy.

Do not assume that cloud synchronization or automatic backups are enabled unless the feature is available and configured.

## 9. Important Limitations

Daily Khata Pro is a record-keeping and calculation aid. App summaries do not automatically replace bank statements, official accounting records, tax documents, payroll records, or other formal documents.

Calculations and estimates depend on the entered values, assumptions, and implemented logic. They are not guarantees of future results or a substitute for qualified financial, tax, legal, or employment advice.

Always verify important information independently.
